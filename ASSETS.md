# Wilson — Art assets

## Kenney Medieval RTS

Wilson uses selected artwork from **Kenney Medieval RTS** as a free visual base for the browser prototype.

- Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/medieval-rts
- Mirror used for reproducible imports: https://github.com/meehow/medievalRTS
- License: Creative Commons CC0 / public-domain dedication
- Attribution: not required; credit to Kenney is included here as good practice.

The deployed Hatchable project stores a local copy of the atlas and license at:

- `public/assets/kenney/medievalRTS_spritesheet@2.png`
- `public/assets/kenney/medievalRTS_spritesheet@2.xml`
- `public/assets/kenney/License.txt`

Wilson-specific simulation, layout, UI, effects, behaviors and data are separate project work.
